# Fee Structure (2027-28)

## Tuition

| Programme | Tuition per credit hour |
| --- | --- |
| BS (all programmes) | Rs. 13,500 |
| MS | Rs. 15,000 |
| PhD | Rs. 16,500 |

## Other dues

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Admission fee (once) | Rs. 32,000 |
| Paper rechecking, per course | Rs. 2,500 |
| Transcript, per copy | Rs. 1,500 |

## Refunds

A student who withdraws from the semester within the first ten days of classes gets a full refund of tuition. The refund falls to 80 percent by day fifteen, 60 percent by day twenty and 50 percent by day thirty. No tuition is refunded after day thirty.

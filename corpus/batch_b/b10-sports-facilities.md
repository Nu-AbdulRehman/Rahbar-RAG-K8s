# Sports Facilities Guide

## 1. Facilities

The campus has a cricket ground, two tennis courts, a basketball court and an indoor table tennis hall. The gymnasium is open from 7 am to 9 pm on weekdays.

## 2. Booking

Courts are booked at the sports office one day in advance, for one hour at a time. Students show the student card when collecting equipment, and return it the same day.

## 3. Teams

University teams for cricket, football and basketball hold trials in the first month of the fall semester. Players selected for inter-university events may get an attendance relaxation for the days of the event, approved by the Head of Department.

# Examination Conduct Rules

## 1. Entry to the examination hall

A student must reach the examination hall at least ten minutes before the paper starts and carry the student card. A student who arrives more than thirty minutes late is not allowed to sit the paper.

## 2. Items not allowed

Mobile phones, smart watches, notes and bags are not allowed at the seat. They are left at the front of the hall at the student's own risk. Only a non-programmable calculator is allowed, and only when the paper says so.

## 3. Unfair means

Copying, talking to another student, or having any written material at the seat is an unfair means case. The invigilator takes the answer book, records the case and reports it to the Disciplinary Committee, which may cancel the paper or the whole semester.

## 4. Leaving the hall

No student may leave the hall during the first hour of the paper. A student who leaves may not return.

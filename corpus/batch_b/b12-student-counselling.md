# Student Counselling Service

## 1. Who can use it

Every enrolled student can see the campus counsellor free of charge. Sessions are confidential, and nothing discussed is shared with teachers or parents without the student's written consent, except where someone's safety is at risk.

## 2. Appointments

Appointments are booked by email or at the counselling office in the student affairs block, from Monday to Friday, 9 am to 4 pm. A first session lasts forty-five minutes.

## 3. Exam stress

During the final examination weeks the service runs walk-in hours every afternoon, so students under exam stress can see a counsellor without an appointment.

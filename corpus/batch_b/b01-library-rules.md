# Central Library Rules

## 1. Membership

Every enrolled student is a member of the Central Library for the duration of the degree. The student card is the library card and must be shown at the entrance desk.

## 2. Borrowing

An undergraduate student may borrow up to four books at a time for fourteen days. A graduate student may borrow up to six books for twenty-one days. Reference books, theses and journals may be read only inside the library.

## 3. Late returns

A late return is charged Rs. 50 per book per day. Borrowing is suspended while any fine is unpaid, and the degree is not issued until every library due is cleared.

## 4. Conduct

Silence is kept in the reading halls. Food is not allowed beyond the entrance lobby. Group discussion is allowed only in the four discussion rooms, which can be booked at the circulation desk for two hours at a time.
